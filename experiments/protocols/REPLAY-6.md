# FLASHGUARD_REPLAY/6

Supersedes `/5` for new comparisons.

The motion corpus and 45 px/s smooth-versus-snapped scrolling stimulus are unchanged from `/5`. The material change is status isolation: REPLAY/6 pass/fail covers replay motion, static fidelity, direct flash-reduction, and NVOFA execution only. The separate WCAG-oriented flash sweep no longer determines the REPLAY status.

Flash conformance-oriented regression is reported independently by `FLASHGUARD_FLASH_SWEEP/6` / `WCAG_FLASH/5`.

This prevents future evaluator-definition changes from changing the meaning of an otherwise identical motion replay.
